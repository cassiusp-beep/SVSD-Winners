# The brief

Source: "Design Brief: The Search for Top Young Design Talent," released by SVSD on October 15, 2026. This file is the source of truth. If any other doc disagrees with it, this file wins.

## The challenge

> The way we build software has changed. In the past, building a profitable software product often meant designing for a large group of people. Today, it's possible to build for much smaller, more specific audiences.
>
> **Pick a specific group of people and design a product they'll love, even if others might hate it.**
>
> There's just one constraint: **Your product must center around the theme of "storytelling."**

Storytelling can be interpreted however we like. The brief's examples are a reading experience, a presentation tool, a motion graphics editor, or a way to preserve family stories across generations. It is left open so each team ends up with its own portfolio project.

Two things follow from this wording:

- **A narrow audience is the point.** Designing for "everyone" works against the brief. We should name a specific group and accept that others may not like the product.
- **Storytelling must be central, not bolted on.** A judge should be able to say in one sentence how the product is about storytelling.

## Judging criteria

1. **Product thinking.** How we frame the problem, find a compelling solution, and recognize which deeper problems inside the product are worth exploring and solving.
2. **Depth of thinking and communication.** How rigorously we explore ideas, the decisions we make along the way, and how clearly we explain our reasoning.
3. **Craft.** The quality of execution. That covers visual and interaction design, motion, details, and the presentation itself.

## Submission

- A video that tells the story of the project. **7 minutes max.** Up to 10 minutes is allowed only if it is genuinely interesting and entertaining for that long.
- Show the problem, the solution and, most importantly, **how we explored it and the interesting challenges we identified and solved along the way.**
- The format is up to us. It can be a recorded deck, a prototype walkthrough, a website, or a mix. "The way you communicate your work is part of the work."
