# Submission video

**Limit: 7 minutes.** Aim for about 5:30 so there's room. The brief says the way we communicate is part of the work, so the video is judged on craft too.

## Structure

The timings are provisional.

| Time | Section | What it must do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Hook | Open on the product working. One striking moment, before any explanation. |
| 0:30–1:15 | The people and the problem | Name the specific group. Show their real friction. Say why existing tools fail them. |
| 1:15–1:45 | Principles | Two or three principles, one line each. |
| 1:45–4:30 | The product | Walk the hero path. Show each key decision and the principle behind it. |
| 4:30–5:30 | How we got here | Two or three turning points: what we tried, why it failed, what we changed. This is what the brief says matters most. |
| 5:30–6:00 | Close | Return to the person from the opening. End on what is now possible for them. |
