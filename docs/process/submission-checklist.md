## 2. Judging Criteria & Demo Mapping

The competition is evaluated across three core criteria. Each criterion maps directly to a specific segment of our demo and prototype:

### 1. Product Thinking
*How we frame the problem, find a compelling solution, and recognize which deeper problems inside the product are worth exploring and solving.*
- **Problem Framing in Demo:** Creative portfolios (Behance, Dribbble, LinkedIn) show static finished trophies, isolating makers from peer collaboration and burying messy exploration.
- **The Solution:** **Branch** turns creative work into a living, branching tree on a single infinite canvas where the process *is* the portfolio.
- **Deeper Problems Solved:**
  - Removing gatekeeping: Anyone can branch an open project directly on the canvas without permissions or pull requests.
  - Transparent collaboration: Organic dialogue lines carry real discussion and attribution directly between nodes.
  - Asking for help without friction: Glowing gap nodes act as beacons for collaborators.

### 2. Depth of Thinking & Communication
*How rigorously we explore ideas, the decisions we make along the way, and how clearly we explain our reasoning.*
- **Recorded Turning Points (`DECISIONS.md`):**
  - Turning Point 1: Rejected traditional feed and card grids (anti-slop) in favor of a single spatial infinite canvas centered on the user.
  - Turning Point 2: Eliminated modals, sheets, and tabs so users never lose spatial orientation; context blooms directly in place.
  - Turning Point 3: Proximity-based morphing and hot corners replace top-heavy navigation bars.
- **Story Structure in Video:** Led by crisp design principles (*Exploratory by design*, *AI as an option*, *The project is the story*). Clear narrative of testing, failing early, and arriving at the spatial canvas model.

### 3. Craft
*The quality of execution: visual design, interaction design, motion, details, and presentation.*
- **Visual Design:** Hand-drawn marker aesthetics, saturated ink palette, deliberate white space, tactile typography, and elimination of generic AI slop.
- **Interaction & Motion:** Organic bulbing radial actions, fluid spring physics, live bezier dialogue links, and magnetic drag morphing.
- **Responsive Craft:** Validated at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 touch viewports with dedicated gesture adaptations.
