## User-supplied material

[Product Direction Notes.png](Product%20Direction%20Notes.png) supplies the ideas of multiple inputs, personal capture friction, collaborative living maps and shared context for agents. Those notes are concepts to investigate, not evidence that meetings are routinely recorded or that maps improve corporate efficiency.

The user’s clarification sets the audience: **individuals first, with corporate communication as an apparent expansion path; do not center the product on students.**

## Working conclusion

Continue with a narrow prototype of a persistent, editable, source-linked visual memory. Test whether it helps an individual recover and reuse thinking after time has passed, with a manageable review burden. The proposed advantage lies in that workflow and information continuity; it has not been demonstrated through user research. Shared maps and agent context should extend that value only after the individual experience works.

No claims of market size, adoption, retention, time savings, willingness to pay or exclusive novelty are made in this package.


## Collective-emergence pivot — 18 October 2026

This update supersedes the individual-first audience and working conclusion above. See [the current product definition](../../context/product-definition.md). The user now proposes a live shared semantic map for teams. The recommended early audience is multidisciplinary design/engineering teams during concept development; underserved demand remains unvalidated.

- [Ben Shaw, supplied 2009 presentation](Collective%20Emergence%20-%20Ben%20Shaw%202009.pdf): engineering design vignettes distinguish combination/transformation and attribute emergent outcomes to contributions layered across participants. PDF pages 9–14 are the relevant examples and synthesis. The deck does not evaluate a live AI mapping app or establish causal gains in creativity. The final added page is a screenshot of a C–K theory article, not a Branch experiment.
- [Granola for Product](https://www.granola.ai/use-cases/product): already targets product teams; “for teams” alone is not a new market. Granola is the familiar capture analogy, not evidence of an unserved demographic.
- [Otter](https://otter.ai/): advertises transcription, live assistance and follow-ups. Do not describe it as only a static text output.
- [Mapify](https://mapify.so/): already advertises recorded audio/content-to-map conversion. A visual summary alone is not differentiation.
- [MeetMap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01564): real-time collaborative dialogue mapping with LLMs, evaluated with 20 participants in ten pairs. This is close prior art. Its automation/agency findings motivate a test; they do not validate Branch's team niche or establish product-market fit.

The proposed opportunity is a participant-editable history of alternatives, constraints and idea transformations, with careful live interaction and explicit review of consequential meaning. Competitors' inability to copy this has not been shown. Use evidence-linked expressed contributions for within-person/across-person comparisons; inferred relationships do not prove mental states or causal influence.
